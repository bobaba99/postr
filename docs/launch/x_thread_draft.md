**1/6**
I built Postr — a free academic poster tool for students and researchers.

No signup. No PowerPoint. Open the editor, start typing, it autosaves.

postr.sh

---

**2/6**
The premise: conference posters don't need 400 fonts or a freeform color picker.

They need 6 legible fonts, discipline-appropriate palettes, and a 3-column grid that actually prints at 48×36.

Constraint as feature.

---

**3/6**
What's in the box:
- Templates tuned for APA, SfN, and generic 3-column layouts
- Live reference manager with APA / MLA / Vancouver styles
- Palette designer — paste hex codes or drop an image to extract
- Greek + math shortcuts (\\alpha, \\sum) as you type

---

**4/6**
Every keystroke autosaves — from before you've named the poster. Sign up later and your drafts follow you across devices. No "export and re-import" to migrate an anonymous session.

---

**5/6**
Print-ready out of the box. Built-in readability audit flags body text that won't be legible from three feet away — before you pay the print shop.

---

**6/6**
Free for students. No ads, no watermark.

Would love feedback from anyone presenting at a conference this spring.

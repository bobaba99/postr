      My bottom line  The report confirms Postr's wedge is real but the window is visibly closing — BioRender shipped a Poster Builder (Jun–Jul 2026), SciSpace owns the Paper2Poster fork, and every major player added an MCP server in the last 12 months. Postr's defensible moat isn't generation (commoditized at ~$0.005/poster) — it's the edit + print last mile, trust (extract from the manuscript, never fabricate citations — the exact failure that sinks Gamma and SciSpace with academics), and the $18.99 no-auto-renewal term against a field where billing rage is the #1 complaint in six of nine profiles.

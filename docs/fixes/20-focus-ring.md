# Record 20 — The focus ring after a mouse click: keyboard navigation only

**Source:** plan item 20 (`docs/stress-test/PLAN.md`), from record 03's owner question "A ring after a mouse click" (`docs/fixes/03-fit-whole-sheet.md`, cause D and section 10; the owner kept the item on 2026-09-30) · **Branch:** `fix/20-focus-ring`, off main `ac7d793` · **Status:** implemented; reproduced first from the user's entry with the committed instrument (`apps/web/scripts/fit-check.mjs`, new scenarios `focus-*` in `lib/focusRingScenarios.mjs`) in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit; reviewed (the one round of a simple feature: the browser, through the user's entry points, on `focusring-frozen-1`; 6 findings, 2 MEDIUM, 3 LOW, 1 INFO) and corrected (correction 1, section 9: the `<summary>` disclosures added to the pointer-mode rule, R20-1; the first build's rule M3 removed, so Enter and Esc after a click never switch the ring on, R20-2; four sentences made true, R20-4, R20-5; the limits the round measured recorded, R20-3, R20-6); the correction is in the frozen copy `focusring-frozen-2`.

Evidence labels as in `docs/fixes/README.md`: MEASURED (a number from a re-runnable command), TESTED (a test that fails without the change), INSPECTED (read, nothing ran), UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Symptom

A researcher clicks **Zoom in** (or FIT, the % readout, a sidebar tab, Save PDF in the top bar, Hide sidebar, Auto-Arrange, a palette), then presses an arrow to nudge a block, holds Shift to Shift+click another one, or presses Enter, Space, a letter or Esc. A purple ring appears around the button she clicked, as if she had tabbed to it, and stays until she clicks somewhere else. In Chrome only (Chromium; Edge shares the engine, not measured). The same with the browser's own blue ring on a link, a checkbox or a disclosure line (the Edit tab's "💡 Tips for editing tables"; Safari draws its own there too), and on the button a dialog focuses when she opens it again with Enter or closes it with Esc (review round 1, R20-1 and R20-2). A keyboard user's ring (Tab to a button) must stay as it is.

## 6. Root cause

- **Hm (H1).** The ring is drawn by `:focus-visible` rules: the house ring (`index.css`, `button:focus-visible, [role="button"]:focus-visible`, `outline … !important` since record 03's cause D, so it also draws on buttons styled with an inline `all: unset`) and, on other elements, the browser's own `:focus-visible { outline: auto }`. Chromium focuses a button on a mouse press and does not match `:focus-visible` then, but its heuristic turns `:focus-visible` on as soon as a key is pressed while that button has the focus, unless the key is ⌘, Ctrl or Alt, alone or in a chord (MEASURED: ⌘Z 0 of 20, every other key of the eight 20 of 20; review round 1's R20-4, re-measured by `focus-mouse-modifiers` in Chromium through Playwright on macOS: Meta, Alt, Control, ⌘K, Alt+K and Ctrl+K 0 of 6, Shift and F2 2 of 2; the first build called Ctrl UNVERIFIED and said nothing of Alt). Firefox focuses the button too and does not turn it on; WebKit does not focus a button on a click. Before cause D the house rule lost to the inline `all: unset` (record 03 measured 11 of 11 buttons drawing no ring; not re-measured here), so on those buttons a mouse user saw nothing after a key, and a keyboard user saw nothing either.
- **H2, the siblings, share the cause:** Chromium's heuristic, with the browser's own ring instead of the house's, on the two links and the two checkboxes (4 of 6). The menus set `outline: none` inline and draw nothing. Review round 1 (R20-1) found three more of the same: `<summary>` disclosures, which the first build's pointer-mode rule did not list (Chromium: the table tips, the sign-up page's email preferences and the pricing page's "What's included", 20 of 23 click-then-key cases on main and on the first build alike; WebKit, which focuses a `<summary>` on a click though not a button, draws its own there too, section 4), and a table's Show caption checkbox, which the first rule already covered. The first build's sweeps missed them because they ran with nothing selected: the table's tips and its caption box are the Edit tab's, shown with a table selected.
- **H5, the first build's own rule M3:** a mouse user's Enter or Esc after which the page moved the focus (ConfirmModal focusing its first button as it opens, and giving the focus back to its opener as it closes; `components/ConfirmModal.tsx`) switched the page to keyboard mode, and Chromium's `:focus-visible`, on after the key, drew the ring (6 of 6). Not a regression from main, where Chromium drew the same 6 of 6; the plan's rule ("Enter … does not switch it on") excludes it.
- **Siblings looked for and not found:** keys that move the focus as navigation, other than Tab, in the chrome (INSPECTED, every `.focus(` in `apps/web/src`: the dialogs' Enter and Esc as they open and close, the public menu's Esc giving the focus to its button, the table's arrows between its cells, which are text fields on the sheet, and Tab traps; no roving tabindex and no radio group in the app), so dropping M3 takes away no arrow or Home/End navigation a keyboard user has; other `:focus-visible` rules in the app (`grep focus-visible apps/web/src`, MEASURED: the house rule, `[data-focus-inset]` and the sidebar tabs' `outline-offset` only, the code box's textarea ring, and one Tailwind `focus-visible:outline` class on the phone notice's close button, which the house rule's `!important` already overrode); buttons with an inline outline that means something else (none: the one inline `outline` in the editor that is not `none` is on the figure-size overlay, a div); a button whose own corners are neither inline nor 4 px, which the house rule's `border-radius: 4px` would reshape while `:focus-visible` (none in the editor's ten tabs, a scratch probe, `<scratch>/probe4.mjs`, MEASURED; the public pages' menu button is one, 6 px, read by `focus-mouse-public-menu`).
- **What it does not explain.** WebKit's ring on a dialog's button after a mouse click (section 4): WebKit's own rule for a script's focus, not a key; the fix changes it too (section 7). Undo and Redo not keeping the focus after a click (fix 12) and A− / A+ keeping it where it was (record 33): by design, they never had a ring to lose (MEASURED: the focus elsewhere after the click, section 4). Text fields always match `:focus-visible` (the specification's rule for fields that take typing) and keep their own focus styles.

## 7. The change

**The rule** (`apps/web/src/lib/inputModality.ts`, new, its header): the page keeps the last input modality on `<html>` as `data-postr-input-modality`.

| # | When | Then |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | a pointer is pressed anywhere (mouse, pen, touch) | `pointer` |
| M2 | Tab is pressed, with any modifier (Shift+Tab; Option+Tab, Safari's way to buttons), whether or not anything takes the focus | `keyboard` |
| ~~M3~~ | ~~the focus moves to another element after a key, with no pointer pressed since~~ (the first build: Esc giving the focus back to a dialog's opener, Enter opening one) | removed by correction 1 (R20-2): such a key is M4's, the modality unchanged |
| M4 | any other key (the arrows, Shift, Enter, Space, a letter, ⌘ shortcuts, Esc), whatever the page then does with the focus | unchanged |
| M5 | before any input | absent: rings as the browser decides, as before the change |

The listeners are on the window in the capture phase (`pointerdown`, `keydown`; the first build's `focusin` is gone with M3), installed once in `main.tsx` before React renders, so they run before the page's own: an open `ConfirmModal` stops every key in the window's capture phase, and the block controls stop the pointer's propagation (both in the unit tests).

**Why M3 went (correction 1).** The plan's rule names Enter among the keys that, pressed after a click, "do not switch it on", and its gate is "no ring after a click then a non-Tab key"; M3 drew one after Enter or Esc whenever the page moved the focus (H5, 6 of 6 in Chromium). The owner's wording also says "keys that move focus": in this app's chrome no key but Tab moves the focus as navigation (section 6, INSPECTED), so with M3 gone the rule is Tab only, and the keys after which the page itself moves the focus (a dialog's Enter and Esc) are M4's. Keyboard users keep every ring: from the first Tab the page is in keyboard mode and stays there until a pointer press (Hk, section 8). Review round 1's alternative (M3 for the keys that navigate only: the arrows, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown) was not built: it has no subject in this app, and it would keep M3's other edge (a late focus move, such as the window getting the focus back, after an arrow that nudged a block, turned keyboard mode on: R20-6, now TESTED as pointer in jsdom).

**The CSS** (`apps/web/src/index.css`, the focus-visibility section): the house rule is drawn only while the page is not in pointer mode (`:where(html:not([data-postr-input-modality="pointer"])) button:focus-visible`, and the same for `[role="button"]`: its outline and its 4 px corners); a second rule turns the browser's own ring off in pointer mode on buttons, `[role="button"]`, links (`a[href]`), checkboxes and, since correction 1 (R20-1), `<summary>` (`outline: none !important`). `:where()` keeps both at the specificity the house rule had, so nothing that won or lost against it before changes; keyboard rings look the same (section 8: the look counts equal in each engine).

## 10. Limits and follow-ups

- **The ring before any input follows the browser** (M5): a page that has just opened and focuses a button by script, before any click or key, shows the ring where the browser's `:focus-visible` matches (MEASURED, `focus-script-before-input`, 4 of 4 in each engine, as on main). Chosen so that no keyboard user loses a ring at load; a mouse user loses it at the first press.
- **A mouse user's Enter and Esc never switch the ring on** (correction 1, R20-2): a mouse user who opens a dialog with Enter on a button she clicked, or closes one with Esc, sees no ring on the button the dialog focuses or gives the focus back to, until she presses Tab (MEASURED, `focus-dialog-mouse`, 0 of 6 in Chromium after; main and the first build 6 of 6). This is the plan's rule; Chromium's own rule, and the first build's M3, drew it. If the owner wants the ring there, the rule needs to know which keys navigate (UNVERIFIED as wanted; the first build's M3 is the mutant `focus-move-after-key-keyboard`).
- **A keyboard user who clicks once** is in pointer mode until the next Tab: the button the keyboard reached keeps the focus but loses its ring after a click on A− or A+ (record 33: they keep the focus where it was), and Enter then acts on it with no ring (MEASURED, R20-3, `focus-keyboard-then-press`, in each engine: main a ring after the click and after Enter, the fix none). Keyboard-only users lose nothing (the reviewer's keyboard-only probe, 0 rings lost in three engines; Hk, section 8). The way out the review named (pointer only when a press moves the focus, a `focusin` after the press) changes the rule for every click that keeps the focus, and for WebKit, which focuses no button on a click: not built (PLAN's Later list).
- **WebKit's ring on a mouse-opened dialog's button is gone** (section 8, information): Safari users who open a dialog with the mouse no longer see where its focus is; opened from the keyboard (Option+Tab, Enter) it rings as before.
- **Elements outside the rule's selectors:** radios (none in the app), sliders and colour wells (behind `ADJUSTMENTS_ENABLED`, off on this tree), file inputs (`display: none`) and other `[tabindex]` elements (none outside the sheet) keep the browser's behaviour. Switching `ADJUSTMENTS_ENABLED` on brings two that ring after a click and Shift in Chromium: a slider and a colour well (MEASURED, `focus-mouse-siblings` served with the switch on, `POSTR_MUTANT=<scratch>/adjustments-on.json#on`, a one-edit spec that turns `export const ADJUSTMENTS_ENABLED = false;` to `true` in `config/features.ts`: 2 of 17 elements on the fix, 10 of 17 on main). Add `input[type="range"]` and `input[type="color"]` to the pointer-mode rule when the switch goes on: the committed sweep goes red that day, so it cannot pass unseen (PLAN's Later list). The `<summary>` in the figure check's result ("or change one", `poster/ReadabilityPanel.tsx`, on the check panel and /tools/figure-readability) is covered by the same rule and not measured (INSPECTED).
- **Not measured:** real Safari, Windows, a physical keyboard, touch screens with a keyboard (the reviewer's touch probe stands in: Playwright's taps), screen readers (VoiceOver moves the focus with keys the page does not see: the modality stays as it was, absent on a fresh page), Safari's "Press Tab to highlight each item" setting, the guidelines panel and the comments tab (hidden), dialogs other than the template's and the dashboard's Delete, the phone notice's close button (the harness dismisses the notice).

## 1. Symptom

Not a defect report: two owner decisions the copy disagreed with.

1. **"LaTeX export: remove it for now"** ("unnecessary for now"). On main the Export tab offers "⌨ LaTeX source (.zip)" to every plan, and 25 strings in the app, 8 crawler strings and `index.html` tell visitors and buyers that the paid plans unlock "PowerPoint and LaTeX" (the landing card "…or continue in LaTeX.", the paywall "Keep editing in PowerPoint or Overleaf", the profile, `/billing/success`, the size notes that send a too-big poster to LaTeX).
2. **"Stripe prices are before tax."** On main no price says so: the /pricing cards print "CA$18.99" and "CA$9.99" bare, as do the paywall, the `/auth?plan=` banner, the profile's free plan, the account-deletion line and the crawler copy.

Also asked: every "registered in Quebec" in non-legal copy becomes "incorporated in Quebec" (answer 4), and the landing "Editable exports" card says the exports are paid.

## 6. Root cause

Why the decisions touched 30 places, not one: the list of paid exports and the two prices are written out by hand in every surface that mentions them, with no shared source and no test across surfaces. "PowerPoint and LaTeX" was repeated in 25 strings over 12 files plus 9 crawler and `index.html` strings; the prices were typed in 8 places (`PricingSection.tsx`, the paywall, `Auth.tsx` ×2, `SubscriptionPanel.tsx`, `DangerZone.tsx`, `ExportDrawer.tsx`, `routes.json`). The existing tests pinned strings one surface at a time, so a change of decision could only be found by reading every file.

What this does not explain: the missing tax note is not drift; until 2026-10-06 no decision said prices were before tax. And it does not explain the LaTeX button itself, which was a product choice the owner reversed.

The fix keeps the copy where it is (each surface words its own sentence) and adds the cross-surface check instead: the copy inventory.

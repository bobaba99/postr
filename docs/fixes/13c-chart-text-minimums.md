## 1. Symptom

A researcher inserts a chart from the Figure tab (Make a figure › "I don’t have data yet" › a shape › Insert selected figures, or Insert › Chart). The chart lands at 10 × 7 in with its caption under it. The caption, which starts "Sample data, not real results.", is not on the poster: it falls past the block's frame, which clips. So do the x-axis title and, often, the tick labels. The y-axis title's capitals are cut at the top. With the caption beside the chart, or with a legend in a smaller block, every text in the chart shrinks: tick labels print at 11.3 pt where the checker asks for 14. And three surfaces judge figure text against three different sets of minimums: the code check 18/18/14/14/12 pt (warning within 15 %), the image scan 24/24/18/18/18/18 (warning within 25 %), the inserted charts 18/24, which their own comment called "the checker's".

## 6. Root cause

**RC5 — three sets of minimums.** `readability.ts:157-181` (the code check, 18/18/14/14/14/14/12), `ReadabilityPanel.tsx:1019-1026` with a 0.75 band at `:1055` (the image scan, 24/24/18/18/18/18), `plotOptions.ts:60-61` (the charts, 18/24) on main (INSPECTED). The public page and its French twin promise the first set (`i18n/figureReadability.ts`, `seo/routes.json`).

**RC6 — the chart's svg against the block's fixed box.** One mechanism with five parts, all on main:
- (a) drawn at the block's stored size (`ChartBlock.tsx:44-45`) and shown in a smaller box (the border; a side caption's 35 %), so `meet` scales it down (H2);
- (b) the legend added to the svg's height (`renderChart.ts:61, 88, 113`) with a viewBox and `meet` (`:136-137`), so the text shrinks by the legend's share (H1);
- (c) sizes rounded (`renderChart.ts:45`, the legend's font size, the base font) (H3);
- (d) no caption chrome for charts (`blocks.tsx:1672`, `:1886-1908`): the caption took height from a body that is not pinned, and the frame held at the block's height clipped the caption and the bottom axis (H4);
- (e) titles and labels laid out for smaller text or not at all (H5): axis titles enlarged after Plot placed them; category, line-end and long axis labels never wrapped, the left margin sized by a character estimate capped at 42 % (`plotOptions.ts` on main).

**What it does not explain:** tick labels on a continuous or band axis whose glyphs meet (Plot's tick density and single words wider than their band; 61 of 924 x-axis cases on main, section 10); where Insert puts a chart (its open-slot search uses stored sizes and skips the title and authors, `PosterEditor.tsx:1822-1862`); charts missing from the PPTX export; a block far smaller than the range (4 × 3), where even the minimums do not fit.

**Found by review round 1 (section 9), three causes the round-0 fix left:**
- **RC6 (f) — text extents counted, not measured** (Q-R2 to Q-R4): every width the layout used was the character count × 0.6 em (short for capitals and wide digits), and a continuous y axis's left margin was a constant (`plotOptions.ts` round 0: `Math.round(labelPx * 2.6)`, 87 px for "9,000,000" about 118 px wide), a legend entry was placed at its row's start whatever its width, and wrapped category labels were never checked against their band. It also explains part of round 0's own limit above: the band labels that met.
- **RC6 (g) — the floor policy** (Q-R1): when the legend, the margins and a plot of four tick heights did not fit at the minimums, the plot kept its floor and the svg came out taller than its box, and `meet` scaled every text below the minimums; round 0's record had it happen only below the range, which the review's partition refuted (6 × 4.5, and 6.5 × 5 and 7 × 5 beside a side caption).
- **RC7 — a 1.5 px frame border drawn two ways** (Q-R5's side effect, Q-R7): a selected or out-of-bounds frame's border is 1.5 px; Chromium draws borders whole device pixels wide, so on a 1x screen the editor draws it 1 px wide (the chart is drawn for that box) and the print, zoomed 9.6 times, 14 device px, 1.458 units; with the print copy keeping the editor's chrome (`printPoster` stripped only `[data-postr-overlay]`), a chart in such a frame printed 0.94 % (selected) or 1.6 % (out of bounds beside a long caption) smaller than drawn.
- Not explained by these: Q-R6 (Insert's placement, above), Q-R8 (stream P's line), Q-R9 (the caption's lower-casing in `captionFor`).

## 7. Fix

**The minimums, one module (RC5).** `poster/figureTextMinimums.ts`: `FIGURE_TEXT_MIN_PT` (plot title 18, axis titles 18, tick labels 14, legend text 14, legend titles 14, strip text 14, captions 12), `FIGURE_TEXT_WARN_RATIO` 0.85 and `figureTextStatus(pt, minPt)`. Read by:
- the code check: `readability.ts`'s `R_ELEMENTS` and `PY_ELEMENTS` take `minPt` from it (no value and no multiplier changed; the rest of the file is stream P's). Since the merge into fix 13b the tables are in `readabilityTypes.ts`, and the row status is `figureTextStatus` too (Q-R8, record 13b section 11);
- the image scan: `MIN_PT_BY_ROLE` maps titles and axis titles to 18, tick labels, legend, data labels and other text to 14 (the scan cannot tell legend titles or strips apart), and the status is `figureTextStatus`, so the warning band is 15 %, as the panel's legend says ("Up to 15% below the minimum.");
- the charts: `chartLayout.ts` draws at 18 pt (axis titles 24) or the minimums if they were ever higher, and never scales below them.

**Charts lay themselves out inside the box they are drawn in (RC6).**
- (a) `ChartBlock` reads the box its host is laid out in with a `ResizeObserver` (poster units, before the zoom) and renders at it; it waits for that box rather than draw first at the stored size (in WebKit the first draw landed: every chart was drawn twice as the editor opened, section 8); a box of zero (the editor hidden behind Export › Preview poster, whose Print copies that sheet) keeps the last box; a chart on screen stays while it redraws for a new box (no "Rendering chart…" flash). With no layout to report (jsdom, a hidden tree) it draws at the stored size, as before.
- (b, c, e) `charts/chartLayout.ts` (new, pure): the text sizes (not rounded: 25 px and 33.33 px at 10 px per unit), the legend's rows (wrapping at the width), wrapping of labels to a width, the margins (the y title above the plot, a gap and half a tick label at the top; the tick band, wrapped x labels and the x title at the bottom; the widest wrapped category label at the left; the widest line-end label at the right) and the plot's height, the box's height less the legend. Only when the legend, the margins and a plot of at least four tick-label heights do not fit at full size does the text scale, in quarter-point steps of tick text, and never below the minimums (`fitTextScale`); in a box smaller still the plot keeps its floor and the svg comes out taller than the box (only below the range: section 10). `plotOptions.ts` builds Plot's options from it (`buildAt`, laid out at full size first, again at the fitted scale when needed); category labels on the band axes wrap to their room, line-end labels wrap and the right margin holds them, the single stacked bar keeps its short height. `renderChart.ts` sets the axis titles to their size and places their lines on the lines the margins hold (a y title's first baseline 0.95 em below the top, an x title's last 0.3 em above the bottom, wrapping a title too long for the width), and paints the legend above the plot from the rows laid out, anchored at the start (Plot's root sets `text-anchor: middle`, which centred each label on its swatch).
- (d) `blocks.tsx`: `CaptionWrapper` pins a chart's body to the block's height as it does an image's (`isImageLike` includes chart), and the frame grows with any caption or note, side captions included (`chartHasGrowingChrome`: unlike an image, the chart body is pinned beside a side caption too, so a frame held at the block's height would cut its x axis, and a side caption taller than the chart would be cut).

Why this shape: the decision's own list, and the one place each number lives. ### Corrections after review round 1 (findings Q-R1 to Q-R5 and Q-R7, section 9)

One cause behind Q-R2 to Q-R4 (the reviewer's reading, re-measured here): the layout knew text only as a character count at 0.6 em, or not at all (a continuous y axis's left margin was a constant). Q-R1 is a different cause: when even the minimums did not fit, the policy let the svg come out taller than its box. Q-R5's side effect and Q-R7 share a third: a 1.5 px frame border drawn differently by the editor and the print.

- **Text is measured, in the chart's font** (`charts/textMeasure.ts`, new): a canvas measures each string in the poster's font family at the size it is drawn at (after `document.fonts.load` has had up to 3 s to fetch the font), padded by 2 % and 0.16 em, and every width the layout uses comes from it: the legend's entries, wrapping, the left margin of a category axis, line-end labels, titles. At the drawn size because widths do not scale with the size (the system font spaces small text wider: a title measured at 100 px and scaled came out 3 % short at 25 px in Chromium, 4 % in Firefox and WebKit); padded because an engine's SVG text can come out wider than its canvas measures (WebKit's "2024" 59.58 px against 58.79; Firefox's text box 4 px wider than its advance) (MEASURED, scratch probe `ffmeasure`, the three engines, 6 strings at 19.4, 25 and 33.3 px; the first try measured at 100 px and left Firefox with one clipped title and 8 chart-sizes of meeting labels, harness run `f3-firefox`). jsdom has no canvas: there an estimate stands in (capitals 0.75 em, the rest 0.6 em). `ChartBlock` draws the chart again when a face of its font finishes loading (`useFontLoads`), so a chart drawn before the poster's font arrived is laid out in the font it shows.
- **The tick labels Plot drew are read back** (`charts/tickFit.ts`, new; Q-R2): on a continuous axis Plot picks the ticks and their labels, so after it draws, the widest y label sets the left margin, the x labels at the plot's ends set the left and right margins, and x labels closer than their half-widths and half a tick label set a wider tick spacing; `renderChart` lays the chart out and draws it again with them (at most three more times; each override only widens). A "1,000,000" y label and a "12,000" x label at the plot's right end now fit.
- **Labels never wider than their room** (Q-R3, Q-R4): `wrapText` breaks a word wider than its room after its hyphens, then between its characters; a legend label longer than its row wraps onto lines of its own and its row is that much taller (the painter draws the lines); a category label's room on the left widens from 42 % of the width up to 60 % to hold its widest word whole ("MINDFULNESS-" beside a side caption); a category axis's title, centred under the plot, wraps to twice the room between its centre and the nearer edge; the y title above the plot stops short of the line-end labels' margin; and where a band under the plot cannot hold even one character of its labels (ten years under a heatmap beside a side caption, 14 px apart and each 14 px wide; Firefox's text box, 4 px wider, made them meet: harness run `f4-firefox`), every k-th band is labelled, each label with k bands of room (`bandBelowLabels`); the fit keeps the full-size layout's k at smaller text, where each label then wraps onto no more lines than the fit counted (letting k change with the scale made the heatmap of ten series names beside a side caption at 6 × 4.5 come out taller than its box, ticks at 13.79 pt, in all three engines: harness run `f5-chromium`).
- **Each band holds its label's lines** (Q-R4): a category axis on the left raises the plot's floor to (count + 0.2) bands of its tallest label's stack, font box to font box (Plot sets a wrapped label's lines 1 em apart; a stack of n lines takes n − 1 em and a 1.25 em font box), so wrapped labels no longer run into each other or into the legend above. The x labels' stack under the plot is counted the same way (it was 1.25 em a line).
- **A chart too small for its text at the minimums grows** (Q-R1): the fit is unchanged (the plot shrinks to its floor at full size, then the text scales in quarter-point steps down to the minimums, 14/18, and the print margin below), and `buildPlotOptions` now also returns the least height the chart takes at that floor (`minHeightPx`: legend, margins and the plot's floor there). `ChartBlock` reports it (`onMinHeight`, poster units) and `BlockFrame` grows the chart's box to it: the frame's height without a caption (that height plus the border), the pinned body under a caption (`CaptionWrapper` `bodyMinH`); the box laid out at that height then fits at the minimums and the svg is as tall as its box, so the viewBox does not scale the text below them. A chart that fits keeps its block's height. The chart's own floor sits 0.5 % above the minimums (`PRINT_HEADROOM`, folded into the design sizes too should the minimums ever reach them): a chart at its floor is drawn exactly for its box, and a box the layout rounds a hair short shows it a hair smaller: in Firefox's print a 45.71-unit box printed 45.70 and its 14 pt text at 13.995 (18 chart-sizes of the first full Firefox run, `f3-firefox`; Chromium printed none smaller), and in the editor a host laid out a fraction of a px short of the least height scaled the text by it (the first try handled this with a half-px slack in the fit, which its browser blind-spot run then could no longer show once the margin was in, BELOW 0 of 57 against 6 of 57 without it: the slack went). This is the decision's "inside the block height" given up only where it cannot hold with every text at the minimums (a long legend or long category labels in a small block): the block grows, as it grows for a caption, rather than print text below the minimums or truncate the legend's names (truncated names can become indistinguishable: "Open-label extension coh…" twice).
- **"⎙ Save PDF" strips the editor's chrome** (`export/stripEditorChrome.ts`, new, shared with the thumbnail capture; Q-R5, Q-R7): resize handles, the selection's controls, the overlays; and a selected or out-of-bounds frame's 1.5 px border is set back to the unselected 1 px transparent one. Not only for its colour: Chromium draws a border whole device pixels wide, so on a 1x screen the editor draws that 1.5 px border 1 px wide (MEASURED, scratch probe `sel`: inline 1.5 px, computed 1 px, the chart's host 98 units, not 97) and the chart is drawn for that box, while the print, zoomed 9.6 times, draws it 14 device px wide, 1.458 units, and the chart printed 0.94 % smaller (18 → 17.83 pt; a 14 pt minimum would print at 13.87). Reset to 1 px, the print draws it 0.9375 units wide, never a smaller box than the editor's.

Why this shape: the reviewer's suggested fixes, taken where they hold the gates without new failure modes (measure the drawn text; read Plot's own ticks; wrap the legend; cap a band's lines by growing its room and its floor); for Q-R1, growth over truncation or a lower floor alone: a lower floor still fails for ten long series beside a side caption (at 3.9 × 4.5 in the legend and the margins alone take about 438 of the 450 render px at 14 pt, by a hand estimate of the widths: INSPECTED, not measured), and only a box that grows holds any legend whole.

**Stopping rule** (`docs/fixes/README.md`): the corrections add mechanisms (text measured in the chart's font, tick labels read back and the chart drawn again, a block that grows with its chart, the print copy's border reset), which is the case for one more round; the lead decides. Frozen copy for it: `charts-frozen-2`.

## 10. Limits and follow-ups

- **A chart too small for its text at the minimums grows its block** (since review round 1; a product choice for the owner): the lead's decision 6 asked for the legend inside the block's height, which cannot hold with every text at the minimums for a long legend or long category labels in a small block. 96 of 757 of the review's pasted chart-sizes grow and 3 of the chooser's and the confirmer's 949, all at 6 × 4.5 to 8 × 6 (68 of the 99 at 6 × 4.5), by up to 5.35 in (median 0.9 in; ten series names as the rows of a heatmap beside a side caption; the chooser's Likert stacked bar beside a side caption at 6 × 4.5 by 0.07 in) (MEASURED, Chromium; Firefox 98, WebKit 99). The frame stays taller than the corner a user drags to, and a grown chart can cover the block under it (ISSUES counts it, as for a caption). Alternatives not taken (INSPECTED): truncating legend names (two can become indistinguishable), moving the legend into the caption, warning instead of growing.
- **Insert places a chart by its stored size.** With its caption now shown, a chart's frame is drawn 12.8 to 18.2 units taller than its 70-unit block (the caption at 10 in wide, and the frame's border), so on the 3-column template the inserted chart's caption covers another block in 7 of 7 inserts (it covered blocks by its stored size on main too: the open-slot search finds no room on a full template and skips the title and authors). ISSUES counts the collision (it measures drawn heights: 4 issues on main, 5 after, in a scratch probe of 4 inserts). Images with captions behave the same on main. Placing by the drawn size (or measuring the caption first) is Later (review Q-R6).
- **An older poster's chart with a caption grows** by its caption's height when opened (the caption was clipped before), and may now cover the block under it; ISSUES flags it (INSPECTED, same mechanism as above).
- **The text still scales at small sizes before a block grows**: 234 of 1706 chart-sizes draw some text under the chart's design size (18 pt, axis titles 24), never under the minimums plus the 0.5 % print margin (MEASURED, smallest tick and legend 14.07 pt, axis title 18.76, in all three engines). The decision asked for the plot to shrink, not the text; it shrinks first, to its floor, then the text, then the block grows.
- **Charts are not in the PPTX export** (the poster writer has no chart case, `export/pptx/writer.ts:657-681`; INSPECTED, on main too). The LaTeX re-enable checklist already lists that LaTeX drops them.
- **A selected chart is not redrawn on a 1x screen**: the selected frame's 1.5 px border is drawn 1 px wide there, so the chart's box does not change (MEASURED, scratch probe `sel`: host 98 units before and 1.5 s after the click); on a 2x screen the border is 1.5 px and the chart is drawn again for a box one unit smaller (INSPECTED, not measured: the harness runs at 1x). The print copy resets the border to 1 px either way, so the chart never prints in a box smaller than it was drawn for.
- **A caption made from an upper-case column name lower-cases its first letter** ("Mean sCORE … by cONDITION"; `captionFor`'s `lower()` in `buildSpec.ts`; review Q-R9, INSPECTED, on main too). Later.
- **For stream P (answered in the merge into fix 13b, record 13b section 11):** the code check's row status used a literal 0.85 (`readability.ts:1216`), equal to `FIGURE_TEXT_WARN_RATIO` (review Q-R8); it now reads `figureTextStatus`.
